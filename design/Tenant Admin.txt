Scope:
- Admin manages only its resolved tenant's products, orders, customers, and settings.
- There is no platform control panel, subscription/plan management, or global customer view.
- Do not show Shipping or Static Pages screens until corresponding persistence and
  calculation/content rules are designed.
- Staff invitations are out of v1 until a secure invitation and expiry model is
  designed. Do not expose a dead-end staff-management screen.
- General settings edit only fields present in db-design.txt. Domain verification
  and custom-domain self-service are not part of this design yet.
- Dashboard sales figures are derived from persisted order totals and must label
  their date range and currency. Inventory follows the simple-product/variant
  source-of-truth rule in db-design.txt.
